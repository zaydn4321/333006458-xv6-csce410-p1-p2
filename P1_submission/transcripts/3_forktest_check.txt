Leak check for 3_forktest.txt (run on the host, over the saved transcript)

$ grep -o 'allocated pid [0-9]*' 3_forktest.txt | awk '{print $3}' | sort -n > alloc
$ grep -o 'forked child [0-9]*'  3_forktest.txt | awk '{print $3}' | sort -n > forked
$ grep -o 'pid [0-9]* exiting'   3_forktest.txt | awk '{print $2}' | sort -n > exited
$ grep -o 'reaped child [0-9]*'  3_forktest.txt | awk '{print $3}' | sort -n > reaped
$ grep -o 'freeing pid [0-9]*'   3_forktest.txt | awk '{print $3}' | sort -n > freed

counts: allocated 62  forked 63  exited 64  reaped 64  freed 63

$ comm -23 forked exited      # forked but never exited
(end)
$ comm -23 forked reaped      # forked but never reaped
(end)
$ comm -23 alloc freed        # allocated but never freed = leak
(end)
$ uniq -d freed               # freed twice = double free
(end)
$ comm -13 alloc freed        # freed but alloc not logged
4
(end)

Why the counts are off by one or two: logging was switched on by dbg
commands that were themselves running at the time.
  pid 3 = "dbg on proc". forked before the mask was set, so only its
          exit/reap show up (that is the extra exit and reap).
  pid 4 = "dbg level trace". forked while the level was still info, so
          its fork logged but allocproc (a trace message) did not. it
          was freed after the level went up, so the free shows.
Every pid forked while logging was on exited, was reaped, and was freed
exactly once.
